## 📅 Session 7: Estill Voice Training (EVT)
**Textbook**: *The Estill Voice Model* (or refer to online Estill resources in /knowledge if available).

We have learned *what* the parts are (Module 2). Now EVT teaches us how to **isolate** and **control** them independently.
The Philosophy: "Everyone has a beautiful voice. You just need to know how to drive it."

### 1. The Core Concept: Attractor States
The body likes to be lazy. It likes to group muscles together (e.g., when you laugh, you might also tighten your abs and constrict your throat).
*   **Attractor State**: Your default, habitual way of using your voice.
*   **The Goal**: Break these habits. Learn to move the Thyroid Cartilage *without* moving the Tongue. Learn to close the cords *without* tightening the throat.

### 2. Key Figures (The Controls)
EVT breaks the voice into 13 "Figures for Voice." Here are the 3 most critical ones for beginners:

#### A. True Vocal Body Cover (Thick vs. Thin)
*   **Thick Folds**: The cords meet fully (top to bottom).
    *   *Sound*: Loud, speech-like, "Chest Voice."
    *   *Feeling*: Buzz in the chest/roof of mouth.
*   **Thin Folds**: The cords meet only at the edges.
    *   *Sound*: Quiet, clear, "Head Voice."
    *   *Feeling*: Small vibration, lighter.

#### B. False Vocal Folds (Retraction)
*   **The Villain**: The False Cords sit right above the True Cords. If they squeeze (Constriction), you sound strained/hoarse.
*   **The Fix (Retraction)**: You must actively pull them apart.
*   *How*: Imagine laughing silently or holding your breath *open* (like preparing to be startled). This creates a safe, open throat.

#### C. Thyroid Tilt (The Siren)
*   **Vertical**: The shield is straight. Standard speech.
*   **Tilted**: The shield rocks forward (CT muscle).
*   *Result*: This stretches the cords and sweetens the tone. It eliminates the "break" between chest and head voice.
*   *Exercise*: The "Dog Whimper" roughly finds this tilt.

### 3. The Recipe
Estill says there is no "Magic Technique." There are just recipes.
*   **Belting** = Thick Folds + High Larynx + Twang + Tilted Cricoid.
*   **Opera** = Thin Folds + Low Larynx + Tilted Thyroid.
*   **Pop/Jazz** = Mid Larynx + Mix of Thick/Thin.

### 📝 Session 7 Quiz (Completed)

#### Q1: The Villain
**When you feel strain, scratching, or "effort" in the throat, which structure is likely squeezing (Constricting)?**

> **Answer: The False Vocal Folds (FVF).**
> They act as a clamp. When they constrict, they hide the true vocal folds and create "noise" (hoarseness). The cure is **Retraction** (Silent Laugh).

#### Q2: The Body
**For a loud, powerful "Speech" quality sound, should the True Vocal Folds be Thick or Thin?**

> **Answer: Thick.**
> Thick folds have more mass vibrating against each other, creating a louder, richer sound (Chest Voice).

#### Q3: The Sweetener
**If I want to smooth out the "break" in my voice and hit high notes easily (without yelling), what should I do with my Thyroid Cartilage?**

> **Answer: Tilt it (Thyroid Tilt).**
> Tilting engages the CT muscle, which stretches the cords naturally. This creates a "Sweet" tone and allows for easier high notes (Head/Mix voice).
