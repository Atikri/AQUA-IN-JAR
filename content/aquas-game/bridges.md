<p>1. Connect islands (circles) with <strong>Bridges</strong>.</p>
<p>2. The number in the island tells you how many bridges connect to it.</p>
<p>3. Bridges can be single or double.</p>
<p>4. Bridges generally run horizontally or vertically and <strong>CANNOT</strong> cross.</p>
<p>5. All islands must be connected in a single network.</p>
<br>
<p><strong>Controls:</strong> Click/Drag between islands to build bridges. Click again to make double or remove.</p>
