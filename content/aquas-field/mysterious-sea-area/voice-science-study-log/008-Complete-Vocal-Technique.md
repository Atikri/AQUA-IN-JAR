## 📅 Session 8: Complete Vocal Technique (CVT)
**Textbook**: *Complete Vocal Technique (Cathrine Sadolin)* -- (Refer to PDF in /knowledge if available).

Estill gave us the ingredients. CVT gives us the **Menu**.
CVT is famous for being "Rule-Based." If you follow the rules of a Mode, you cannot hurt yourself.

### 1. The Three Overall Principles
Before you sing *anything*, you must obey these laws:
1.  **Support**: The continuous resistance of the diaphragm's recoil (we learned this in Module 1).
2.  **Necessary Twang**: Every sound needs a *little bit* of Epiglottic funnel narrowing (Twang) to be safe. Without it, the voice is breathy and unprotective.
3.  **No Jaw Jut / Lip Tension**: The jaw and lips must be free. If you lock them, you lock the throat.

### 2. The Four Vocal Modes (The Gearbox)
You can categorize ALL human vocal sounds into one of these 4 modes.

#### A. Neutral (The Whisper / Helium)
*   **Description**: Soft, breathy, or non-metallic. No "bite" in the sound.
*   **Metal**: Non-metallic.
*   **Volume**: Usually soft (pp) to medium (mf).
*   **Vowels**: All vowels allowed.
*   *Example*: Billie Eilish, Choir boys, Lullabies.

#### B. Curbing (The Moan)
*   **Description**: Restrained, emotional, slightly "held back." "Moaning" or "Crying" quality.
*   **Metal**: Half-metallic.
*   **Volume**: Medium volume (mf).
*   **Vowels**: **RESTRICTED**. You can only sing "OH" (Hungry), "UH" (Hungry), or "IH" (Sit). You CANNOT sing a wide "AH".
*   *Example*: R&B runs, Sam Smith, Emotional ballads.

#### C. Overdrive (The Shout)
*   **Description**: Loud, shouting, calling out "HEY!". Direct and bare.
*   **Metal**: Full-metallic.
*   **Volume**: Loud (f).
*   **Vowels**: **RESTRICTED**. Favors "EH" (Hey) and "OH" (No).
*   *Example*: Freddie Mercury, Broadway Belting (Chest), Rock shouting.

#### D. Edge (The Scream / Twang)
*   **Description**: Sharp, piercing, aggressive. Formerly called "Belting" in CVT.
*   **Metal**: Full-metallic + Extreme Twang.
*   **Volume**: Very Loud (ff) or Very Soft (pp) if skilled.
*   **Vowels**: Favors distinct Twangy vowels like "I" (See) and "A" (Cat).
*   *Example*: Axl Rose, The Wicked Witch, Gospel high notes.

### 3. The Rules of the Road
*   You cannot shift huge gears instantly.
*   **Sweet Spot**: Curbing is the safest mode for bridging the "break."
*   **Warning**: If you try to sing "Overdrive" (Shout) on a pitch that is too high (above C5 for women, G4 for men), you will crash. You MUST switch to "Edge" or "Curbing."

### 📝 Session 8 Quiz (Completed)

#### Q1: The Safety Check
**According to CVT, there are 3 things you must do before making any sound. One is Support, one is No Jaw Tension. What is the third?**

> **Answer: Necessary Twang.**
> Narrowing the epiglottic funnel slightly. This protects the vocal folds by reducing the air flow speed and increasing resonance.

#### Q2: The Limit
**I am singing a loud Rock song. I want to sing a high note with an "AH" vowel (like "Father") in Curbing mode. Is this allowed?**

> **Answer: NO.**
> Curbing has strict vowel limitations. "AH" is too open. To stay in Curbing, you must modify the vowel to "UH" (Hungry) or "OH". If you sing a wide "AH" louder, you will accidentally flip into Overdrive or Edge (or crash).

#### Q3: The Identification
**I am listening to Billie Eilish sing softly with a lot of breath in her tone. Which Mode is she using?**

> **Answer: Neutral.**
> The only non-metallic mode that allows for audible breathiness.
