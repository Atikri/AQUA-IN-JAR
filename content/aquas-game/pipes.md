**Goal**: Rotate the pipe segments so that every pipe is connected to the central Power Source (Light Blue Dot).

- **Tap** a tile to rotate it 90°.
- The pipe will turn **Blue** when connected to power.
- You win when all pipes effectively carry power and there are no leaks.

Levels:
- **Easy**: 5x5 Grid
- **Medium**: 7x7 Grid
- **Hard**: 10x10 Grid
