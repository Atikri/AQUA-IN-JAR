How to cook quinoa & rice

September 23, 2025

Anything’s Possible (https://www.instagram.com/burburywholefoods?igsh=bGpuYTAzMnYwbjR3) P56

We find it to be the easiest way to get the fluffiest result.

  • 2 cups quinoa or rice
  1. Rinse the quinoa or rice using a mesh colander. Add to a small pot and cover with 4 cups of water.
  2. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium-high heat, then decrease the heat to maintain a gentle simmer. Cook with the lid on until the quinoa/rice has absorbed all of the water, about 10 to 20 minutes.
  3. Remove the pot from the heat and let the quinoa/rice steam for 5 minutes. Remove the pot from the lid and fluff the quinoa or rice with a fork.
