<p>1. Color cells in the grid according to the numbers at the side of the grid.</p>
<p>2. Numbers indicate the lengths of consecutive filled lines.</p>
<p>3. There must be at least one empty square between consecutive filled lines.</p>
<br>
<p><strong>Controls:</strong> Click/Tap to Fill (Black). Right-click to Mark X.</p>
