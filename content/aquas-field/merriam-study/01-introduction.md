# Introduction

> "A powerful vocabulary gives you the power of persuasion and the advantage of understanding."

## 1. Overview
This study focuses on **Merriam-Webster's Vocabulary Builder**. 
The goal is simple and quantitative: **Expand the personal lexicon extensively** by mastering the building blocks of the English language.

Unlike meaningful rote memorization, this method relies on **Roots** (Greek and Latin) to unlock the definitions of thousands of unknown words.

## 2. Methodology: The Root System
The book is organized by **Units**, each containing several **Roots**.
-   **The Mechanism**: By learning 1 root (e.g., *bene-* meaning "good"), you unlock access to dozens of words (*benefactor*, *benign*, *beneficiary*).
-   **The Structure**:
    1.  **Root Introduction**: Origin and meaning.
    2.  **Key Words**: 4-8 words derived from that root.
    3.  **Contextual Examples**: How the word is actually used in sentences.
    4.  **Quizzes**: Testing retention at the end of each section.

## 3. Study Goals
| Metric | Goal | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| **Daily Intake** | 1 Unit (8-10 words) | Focus on deep understanding of the *root*. |
| **Weekly Review** | 1 Cumulative Quiz | Test retention of previous 7 Units. |
| **Total Target** | 1,200+ Words | Direct from the book, plus derivatives. |

## 4. Why This Matters for Aquas Field
While *Voice Science* requires specific technical terminology, a broad "General Vocabulary" allows for:
-   **Better articulation** of abstract concepts in study notes.
-   **Nuanced expression** in daily "Thinking Voice" logs.
-   **Reading speed improvement** by reducing "dictionary breaks" when reading complex texts like *Seikel* or *Titze*.

## 5. Daily Routine Template
1.  **Read the Root**: Understand its history (Greek vs. Latin context).
2.  **Analyze the Words**: Don't just read definitions; read the *sample paragraphs*.
3.  **Active Usage**: Write 1 sentence for each new word, relating it to *Voice Science* or *Daily Laws* if possible.
4.  **Quiz**: Complete the short fill-in-the-blank quiz.
