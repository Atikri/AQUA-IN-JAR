* **Tag**: #MusicBiz #AI
* **Summary**:
    - **What it is**: Major music groups are implementing new tiered royalty structures and artist opt-in platforms.
    - **Key Points**: Differentiates between human-made, AI-assisted, and fully AI-generated content.
    - **Future Potential**: Could solve the attribution crisis in AI training datasets.
* **Source**: [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/bernardmarr/2024/10/21/the-10-biggest-music-business-trends-for-2025/)

### 2. Spatial Audio for Privacy in Public Spaces
* **Tag**: #SpatialAudio #Privacy
* **Summary**:

    - **Key Points**: Used in offices and museums to create "sound zones" that don't bleed into neighbors.
    - **Future Potential**: Transforming open-plan living and smart city soundscapes.
* **Source**: [Mello Studio](https://mellostudio.com/audio-trends-2025/)
* **Insight**: We can use this concept for "The Secret Chamber" physical installations.
