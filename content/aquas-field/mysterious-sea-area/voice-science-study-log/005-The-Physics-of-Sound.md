## 📅 Session 5: The Physics of Sound (Acoustics)
**Textbook**: *Master Handbook of Acoustics (Everest)* or *Seikel (Chapter on Acoustics)*.

We have left the biological body. Now we are looking at the invisible product: **Sound Waves**.
To understand voice production, you must understand the medium we are manipulating.

### 1. What is Sound?
Sound is not a physical object; it is a **disturbance** traveling through a medium (air).
*   **Compression**: Air molecules jam together (High Pressure).
*   **Rarefaction**: Air molecules spread apart (Low Pressure).
*   **The Wave**: The molecules don't travel from my mouth to your ear; they bump their neighbors like dominoes. The *energy* travels, not the air itself.

### 2. The Big Two: Physics vs. Perception
We must distinguish between what we can **Measure** (Physics) and what we **Hear** (Perception).

| Physical Property (Mapped) | Perceptual Property (Heard) | Unit of Measurement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Frequency** (How fast?) | **Pitch** (High/Low) | Hertz (Hz) |
| **Amplitude** (How big?) | **Loudness** (Soft/Loud) | Decibels (dB) |

*   **1 Hz** = 1 Cycle per second.
*   **A4 (Concert A)** = 440 Hz (The cords open/close 440 times per second).

### 3. The Secret Sauce: Harmonics (Overtones)
If you play A4 (440Hz) on a piano and a flute, they play the same *Frequency*, but they sound different. Why?
**Timbre (Tone Color).**

*   **Pure Tone (Sine Wave)**: Only one frequency (e.g., a Tuning Fork). It sounds cold and sterile.
*   **Complex Tone (Voice/Instruments)**:
    *   **Fundamental Frequency (F0)**: The lowest note (440Hz). This is the "Pitch".
    *   **Harmonics (H1, H2, H3...)**: Whole number multiples of the fundamental (880Hz, 1320Hz, 1760Hz...).
    *   **Key Concept**: The *mix* of these harmonics determines the "Color" of the sound.
        *   strong high harmonics = Bright/Buzzy.
        *   strong low harmonics = Warm/Dark.

### ⚡ Practical Application: The Spectrogram
Voice scientists use a "Spectrogram" to see sound.
*   **X-axis**: Time.
*   **Y-axis**: Frequency (Pitch).
*   **Color/Darkness**: Amplitude (Loudness).
If you look at your voice, you won't see one line; you will see a "stack" of lines like a ladder. The bottom rung is F0 (Pitch), the upper rungs are Harmonics (Timbre).

### 📝 Session 5 Quiz (Completed)

#### Q1: The Speed Limit
**If my vocal cords vibrate 200 times per second, what is the Frequency in Hertz? And roughly what note is that (for a male/female)?**

> **Answer: 200 Hz.**
> This is roughly **G3** (the G below Middle C). This is a very common speaking pitch for adult females, or a mid-range note for adult males.


#### Q2: The Ingredient List
**A Sine Wave (Pure Tone) has ONLY the Fundamental Frequency (F0). What does the Human Voice have that makes it a "Complex Tone"?**

> **Answer: Harmonics (Overtones).**
> The voice produces a fundamental frequency PLUS an infinite series of whole-number multiples (H1, H2, H3...). This "recipe" of harmonics creates the unique timbre of your voice.


#### Q3: Mapping
**"Loudness" is what we hear. What is the physical property we measure to detect loudness?**

> **Answer: Amplitude (Intensity).**
> Typically measured in Decibels (dB).
