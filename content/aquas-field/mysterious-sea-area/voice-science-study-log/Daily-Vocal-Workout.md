# The Science-Based Vocal Workout
**Goal**: Translate theory into muscle memory.
**Duration**: 15-20 Minutes.
**Frequency**: Daily (Consistently > Intensity).

---

## 🧘 Phase 1: Reset & Align (2 Mins)
*Goal: Fix alignment (Module 9) and reduce Phonation Threshold Pressure (Module 10).*

1.  **Body Scan**: Standard Check.
    *   Ears over shoulders? (Fix "Text Neck").
    *   Sternum high? (Allow ribs to expand).
    *   Jaw loose? (Masseter release).
2.  **Straw Phonation (SOVT)**:
    *   Take a small straw. Put in mouth.
    *   Hum a steady tone. Feel the back-pressure expanding your throat.
    *   *Science*: This pushes the vocal folds apart, massaging off any edema.

## 🤸 Phase 2: Stretch & Range (3 Mins)
*Goal: Activate the CT muscle and smooth the transition (Module 2, 4).*

1.  **The Ghost Siren**:
    *   On a "WOO" vowel (lips round, jaw dropped).
    *   Slide from your lowest note to your highest squeak and back down.
    *   *Focus*: Do NOT get louder at the top. Keep it small.
    *   *Science*: We are isolating the **CT Muscle** (the stretcher) without engaging the TA (the thickener).

## 🦾 Phase 3: Strength & Isolation (5 Mins)
*Goal: Control the Figures (Module 7 - Estill).*

1.  **The Silent Laugh (Retraction)**:
    *   Laugh silently ("Hah!"). Feel the throat open wide.
    *   Hold that openness while singing a clear "AH".
    *   *Science*: Retracts False Vocal Folds.
2.  **Thick vs. Thin Onsets**:
    *   **Thick**: Say "UH!" (like being punched). Feel the clean, hard closure.
    *   **Thin**: Say "Who..." (softly). Feel the edges touching.
    *   *Exercise*: Alternate them. "Uh" (Thick) -> "Who" (Thin).

## 🎤 Phase 4: Mode Training (5 Mins)
*Goal: Apply CVT Modes (Module 8).*

1.  **Neutral (Lullaby)**: Sing a phrase breathily. "Fly me to the moon..."
2.  **Overdrive (The Call)**: Call out "HEY!" or "NO!" on a medium pitch.
    *   *Rule*: Keep the vowel "EH" or "OH". Do NOT smile.
3.  **Edge (The Witch)**: Do the "Nasty Witch" sound ("Nyeh!").
    *   *Science*: Narrow the Aryepiglottic Sphincter (Twang) to boost the 3kHz formant.

## 🧊 Phase 5: Cool Down (3 Mins)
*Goal: Flush lactic acid (Module 9).*

1.  **Descending Hum**:
    *   Start high and slide down to your "fry" (lowest creak).
    *   gentle, lazy, quiet.
    *   *Why*: Returns the muscles to their resting length.
