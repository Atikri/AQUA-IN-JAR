# 🎓 Daily Guide: Vocal Biomarker Analysis

> "To understand sound is to understand the movement of the soul."

## 1. The Landscape (Understanding)
*Why is this relevant now? What are the future trends?*
Vocal Biomarker Analysis is the frontier where voice science meets preventative medicine. In 2025-2026, we are moving from "voice assistants" that obey commands to "health assistants" that listen for disease. Your voice carries subtle micro-tremors (jitter and shimmer) that reveal the state of your nervous system, heart, and lungs. Major tech companies are racing to integrate this into wearables, meaning "Voice Health" will soon be a standard metric on your smartwatch alongside heart rate.

## 2. Core Pillars
### 🧠 Knowledge Points
- **Acoustic Features**: Understanding `Pitch` (f0), `Jitter` (frequency perturbation), `Shimmer` (amplitude perturbation), and `HNR` (Harmonics-to-Noise Ratio).
- **Physiology**: How tension in the laryngeal muscles correlates with stress (cortisol levels) and neurological conditions like Parkinson's.
- **The "Vocal Fingerprint"**: The unique combination of prosody, speed, and pause duration that defines an individual's baseline.

### 🛠️ Skills Required
- **Signal Processing**: Basics of `Python` libraries like `Librosa` or `Parselmouth` (Praat interface).
- **Data Hygiene**: ensuring high-quality audio capture (sample rate, bit depth) to avoid analyzing room noise instead of voice.
- **Pattern Recognition**: Ability to distinguish between emotional variance (temporary sad mood) and pathological variance (clinical depression).

## 3. Step-by-Step Operational Guide (MVP)
*Goal: Perform a basic "Vocal Check-up" by the end of the day.*

**Step 1: Preparation (15 mins)**
- Download **Praat** (free scientific software) or set up a Python environment with `pip install librosa`.
- Find a quiet room with no echo. Use the best microphone you have (even a modern phone mic is okay for a start, but a condenser mic is better).

**Step 2: Execution (30 mins)**
- **Task A (Sustained Vowel)**: Record yourself saying "aaaaa" for 5 seconds at a comfortable pitch. Keep it as steady as possible.
- **Task B (Reading)**: Read a standard passage (like the "Rainbow Passage") to capture natural prosody.
- **Analysis**:
    - In Praat/Librosa, extract the **Jitter** (instability in pitch). High jitter often correlates with lack of control or pathology.
    - Extract **Shimmer** (instability in volume).
    - Measure **Speech Rate** from the reading passage. Slowing down can be a marker of cognitive load or fatigue.

**Step 3: Review & Refine (15 mins)**
- Compare your AM (morning) voice to your PM (evening) voice.
- Did your jitter increase after a stressful meeting? This is your personal "Stress Biomarker".
