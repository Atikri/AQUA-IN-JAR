<p>1. Place a <strong>Tent</strong> next to each <strong>Tree</strong> (orthogonally).</p>
<p>2. Each Tent belongs to one Tree.</p>
<p>3. <strong>NO</strong> two Tents can touch, even diagonally.</p>
<p>4. The numbers indicate how many Tents are in that row or column.</p>
<br>
<p><strong>Controls:</strong> Click/Tap to cycle (Empty → Tent → Grass). Right-click for Grass.</p>
