### How to use this tool

The **Wheel of Life** is a popular coaching tool used to help you understand how balanced or fulfilled your life is in this moment. 
1.  **Rate:** Use the sliders to rate your satisfaction level (1-10) for each life area.
2.  **Visualise:** Watch the wheel update in real-time. A jagged wheel means your ride through life might be a bit bumpy right now.
3.  **Reflect:** The tool will automatically highlight the areas you should focus on improving.
